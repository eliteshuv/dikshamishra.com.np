What to wear, where to go, and everything worth knowing before the festivities begin.

01 · Dress with joy

Colour is always welcome.

Traditional Nepali or Indian clothing is warmly encouraged, but never required. Choose breathable layers for the afternoon and bring a light wrap for cool Kathmandu evenings.

Please reserve pure white for ceremonial accents rather than a full look.

02 · Getting here

Meet us in Kathmandu.

Tribhuvan International Airport is the closest airport. Traffic can be lively, so allow at least 45–60 minutes between central Kathmandu locations during celebration hours.

03 · At the table

Come hungry.

Expect a generous Nepali feast with vegetarian and non-vegetarian choices. Please share allergies or dietary needs in your RSVP so the kitchen can take care of you.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

May I bring children?

Yes—this is a family celebration. Please include children in your final guest count.

Will the events be outdoors?

Parts of the Haldi, wedding, and reception are planned outdoors. A covered arrangement will be available.

When should I arrive for the ceremony?

Please arrive by 5:30 PM on 25 April. The auspicious ceremony begins at 6:30 PM.

Can I take photographs during the rituals?

Absolutely, but please keep the central aisle clear and avoid flash during the sacred-fire ceremony.

What about accessibility?

Step-free access and seating support can be arranged. Mention any needs when you RSVP.
