# Couture Design System — dikshamishra.com.np

## Visual Lineage

Designed to match the finalized **Bride's Grand Couture Invitation Card** (`Dr. Diksha Mishra weds Dr. Aashik Jha`):

- **Palette:** Royal Ivory & Blush Parchment (`#FBF2EA`, `#FFFDF9`), Deep Royal Wine (`#5A0F24`), Sacred Vermillion (`#981B2B`), Metallic Gold Foil (`#E5BA63`, `#B88228`), Haldi Gold (`#FFB703`), Sangeet Rani (`#E84A8A`), and Mehendi Mint (`#40C092`).
- **Typography:** `Cinzel` (royal headers & schedules), `Great Vibes` (couture calligraphic titles), `Cormorant Garamond` (editorial prose), `Noto Serif Devanagari` (Sanskrit shlokas & Nepali interface), and `Plus Jakarta Sans` (interactive pills & forms).
- **Assets (`assets/couture/`):** Optimized WebP palace backdrops, alpha-preserved RGBA watercolor cutouts, 1200×630 OpenGraph preview card (`og_preview.jpg`), and 192 kbps stereo MP3 master soundtrack (`bride_soundtrack.mp3`).
